# Journalist fact sheet

For a reporter who may print these numbers, possibly without the surrounding
prose.

Every figure in `key_figures` carries the record it came from, because the
figures are the part most likely to be reprinted alone. Give the figure as it
should be printed and put the unit where the value does not carry one.

`caveats` is required and is the most important field in this document. The
failure mode here is not a wrong number; it is a correct number printed without
the limitation that makes it meaningful. Draw on what the methodology states
about its own limits: that percentiles are statewide, that exposure is modelled
rather than measured at any address, that confidence varies by hexagon, that
demographics are recorded and never scored, and that the score is not a finding
of wrongdoing by any operator.

Do not write a narrative that implies causation the data does not establish, and
do not characterise any operator's conduct, knowledge or intent. A reporter who
wants that will ask the operator, which is their job and not yours.
