# Agency complaint draft

An administrative complaint to a federal or state office. **Not a lawsuit.**

If this is a Title VI disparate-impact complaint, the recipient is EPA's
External Civil Rights Compliance Office and the document must read as an
administrative complaint throughout. Do not use the vocabulary of litigation:
no plaintiff, no defendant, no cause of action, no prayer for relief, no court.
The complainant asks an agency to open an investigation.

`legal_basis` takes statute sections only, and only ones in the retrieved
passages. A complaint proceeds under a provision, not under a dataset record.

Set out the facts as the data records them, each with its record cited. State
what the cited provisions require. Do not argue that the facts establish a
violation: that is the finding the office you are writing to exists to make,
and asserting it is both legal advice and a conclusion you cannot support.

`relief_sought` is what the office can do: open an investigation, conduct a
compliance review, require a corrective action plan. Not damages, not an
injunction, not anything a court awards.
