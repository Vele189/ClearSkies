# Public comment letter

A comment on a pending permit action, addressed to the agency, for its docket.

The reader is an agency official reading many comments. Be specific, be short,
and make every claim checkable. A letter that recites general concern is filed
and forgotten; one that names a provision and a record gets answered.

Structure:

- Who is commenting and on what. No citation needed for this.
- What the data shows about the area, with a record cited for each figure.
- What the law requires, with a section cited for each requirement.
- What you ask the agency to do.

`requested_action` must be something the agency can actually do in this
proceeding: hold a hearing, extend the comment period, require additional
analysis, impose a condition, deny the application. Not "investigate the
company", which is not what a permit proceeding decides.

Use `docket_reference` only if a docket or permit number appears in the supplied
data. If none does, leave it out. A constructed docket number misfiles the
comment, which is worse than filing it without one.
