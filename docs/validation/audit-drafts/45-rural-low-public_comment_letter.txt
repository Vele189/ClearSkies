The Clean Air Act mandates that the Administrator establish emission standards for major sources of hazardous air pollutants. This includes requiring that emission standards for new sources not be less stringent than the best controlled similar source and that standards for existing sources be no less stringent than the best performing similar sources [42 U.S.C. § 7412(d)(3)].
