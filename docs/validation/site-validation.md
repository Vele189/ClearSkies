## How this result has moved, and why

Three runs are worth comparing, because two of the changes between them were
bug fixes rather than methodology and the gate moved both ways.

| | 13 indicators (run 6) | 15 indicators (run 7) | recency fixed (run 11) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary sites | 7 of 10 | 6 of 10 | **7 of 10** |
| Negative controls | 1 of 4 | 1 of 4 | **2 of 4** |
| Confident hexes | 4,793 | 6,093 | **17,263** |

**Adding E1 and E2 cost a pass.** Nearly every site improved — Reserve 95.9 to
97.2 and from 2 scored cells to 6, Norco 96.7 to 98.1, Plaquemine 98.7 to 99.1 —
and Geismar became measurable for the first time. But Chalmette fell 13.9 points
to 83.0 and took the pass with it. Percentiles are relative, and a statewide
modelled surface lifted other places more than it lifted Chalmette. That is not
evidence that E1 and E2 should be removed.

**Fixing the recency term recovered it, and one control.** §12's recency term
was computing as zero for every hexagon, holding confidence below 0.55
statewide. With it fixed, Welcome/5th District and Mandeville had enough
confident cells to be evaluated at all, and both passed. No percentile changed.

## The negative controls are the more serious failure

| Control | run 11 | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Mandeville | 35.4, every cell below the 50th | pass |
| South Lafayette | 41.3, every cell below the 50th | pass |
| Old Metairie | 86.9, all 7 cells at or above the 50th | **fail** |
| Bocage | 95.2, 4 of 7 cells at or above the 50th | **fail** |

A control passes only if the score declines to flag any part of it. Two affluent
neighbourhoods rank in or near the top decile.

The component breakdown for Bocage's anchor cell shows where it comes from:
Population Characteristics is correctly low — `sensitive` 33.8 and
`socioeconomic` 38.8 — while `env_effects` is 86.1, because Baton Rouge has
facilities within the 10 km interaction radius of every neighbourhood in it,
affluent or not. The population half is working. The pollution half is not
distinguishing between living near industry and being burdened by it.

§13.5 rules out the obvious remedy. Moving Environmental Effects from 0.5 to 1.0
leaves a 0.978 rank correlation and dropping the group entirely leaves 0.960, so
reweighting it would change almost nothing. The leave-one-out check points
instead at Sensitive Populations, where S1 or S2 alone moves a quarter of the
state. Both are §8 questions for the methodology paper.

## The three sites still failing

- **Chalmette**, 83.0. Scored on all 7 cells. Fell when the modelled surface
  was added; worth understanding before anything is concluded from it.
- **Gordon Plaza**, 85.2. Scored on all 7 cells, and the nearest to passing it
  has ever been. A residential subdivision built on a former municipal landfill:
  the burden is in the soil, and this score measures air.
- **Geismar**, 85.2 on 2 of 19 cells. The other 17 fall below §5's 25-person
  threshold — the cells average 2.9 residents, because it is a petrochemical
  complex where almost nobody lives. A score that measures burden *on people*
  has little to say there by construction, which is worth stating plainly rather
  than treating as a data gap.
