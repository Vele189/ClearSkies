**Welcome / 5th District (91.0 to 60.6) now fails, and on confidence, not on
score.** Seven of its cells score at the 91.4th–94.2th percentile and every one
is in the Insufficient band, so §12 removes them. The term that removes them is
`c_spatial`, which AUD-05 also changed: it used to be handed the coefficient of
variation of the total-population estimate, and now takes the share of the
hex's population drawn from the seven ACS rate estimates with a CV above 0.30.

against 4,070 with the new one, so the change accounts for the whole of the
drop in the confident universe. The code reproduces the stored term exactly
for all 19,881 scored hexes; this is the specified rule doing what it says,
and `accuracy-review-2026-10-19.md` §5 records why that rule is a methodology
question rather than a defect.
